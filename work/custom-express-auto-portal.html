Custom WordPress Plugin

Express Auto Portal

A custom WordPress plugin that turns a DMV registration business into an online service: customers apply in six steps, upload documents, pay with Stripe and track their application, while the owner manages every case from a private dashboard.

Client

Auto Express Tags (California DMV registration service)

The Problem

The business handled 20+ DMV services by phone and email, with paperwork, fees and status updates spread across calls and inboxes. No ready-made form or booking plugin could handle service-specific fields, document uploads, fee breakdowns, staff-only notes and customer tracking in one flow.

What I Built

  • Six-step application wizard with a progress bar: service, applicant, vehicle, service details, documents, review
  • 20+ services in 6 groups (DMV registration, title, dealer, fleet, VIN, insurance), each with its own detail fields
  • Custom post type for applications with a unique tracking number per case
  • Automatic customer account creation, a secure set-password link and a customer dashboard with application history
  • Two custom roles (Customer and Owner) with wp-admin blocked and the admin bar hidden for portal users
  • Owner dashboard with filters and search by name, email, phone or tracking number
  • Nine application statuses (Pending Review to Completed) with public notes for customers, internal notes for staff and a full status history
  • Public tracking page so customers check progress with their tracking number
  • Stripe Checkout with a fee breakdown (service price, BPA fee, convenience fee, shipping method) and a signed Stripe webhook that marks the application paid
  • HTML email engine: confirmation to the customer, alerts to every owner, status-change and payment emails
  • Encrypted settings screen (AES-256-GCM) for a third-party DMV quote API connection, with secrets never shown in the admin
