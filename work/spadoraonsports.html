Spadora On Sports

A website for a nationally syndicated sports talk show with 30+ years on air.

IndustrySports Media & Podcast
LocationUSA
CategoryArts, Music & Sports
My RoleWordPress Developer

The Challenge

The show needed a home for its podcast episodes, interviews and sponsorship information.

What I Built

  • Latest podcasts section with an Elementor music player
  • Interviews and Affiliates pages
  • Where to Listen section
  • Sponsorship page
  • Gallery and contact form

How It Was Built

  1. Requirements and sitemap reviewed with the project team
  2. Page designs prepared and approved (Figma / PSD)
  3. Approved design built in WordPress with Elementor Pro, section by section
  4. Plugins installed and configured: Music Player for Elementor, WPForms
  5. Responsive check and fixes for desktop, tablet and mobile
  6. Speed, basic on-page SEO and cross-browser testing
  7. Demo review, client feedback round, then launch on the live domain
