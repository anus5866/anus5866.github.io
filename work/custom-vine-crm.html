Custom PHP CRM + WordPress Integration

Vine CRM

A standalone PHP CRM for a facility services company, running next to its WordPress website on the same hosting. Website leads flow from a WPForms form straight into the CRM through a signed webhook, where the team assigns, tracks and follows up on them, and clients get their own portal.

The Vine Facility Services

The Problem

Leads from the website landed in an inbox and got lost, and the company had no system to assign them to staff, control who sees what, or give commercial clients access to their own property records. The CRM also had to stay separate from WordPress so a plugin issue could never expose client data.

What I Built

  • Standalone PHP application outside public_html, with only a small public entry folder on a CRM subdomain
  • WPForms to CRM integration: the WordPress form sends each lead to a webhook signed with HMAC, a timestamp and a one-time nonce, so fake or replayed requests are rejected
  • Lead management with notes, an activity timeline, lead sources and statuses
  • Lead assignment to team members with assignment history and email notifications
  • Five staff roles (CRM Administrator, Operations Manager, Lead Manager, Account Executive, Reporter) with granular permissions
  • Team management with password reset tokens and a permissions screen
  • Client portal with email-invited client users, their assigned properties and CSV export
  • Security: CSRF tokens, login rate limiting, audit logs and prepared statements through PDO
  • Database built from nine versioned SQL migrations
  • SMTP email delivery with PHPMailer for admin alerts and assignment emails
