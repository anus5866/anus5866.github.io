Boston Hills Resort & Hotel

A website for a resort and hotel with rooms and suites, a large event and conference center, a restaurant, pool and airport shuttle service.

IndustryHotel & Event Center
LocationAnambra State, Nigeria
CategoryHospitality
My RoleWordPress Developer

The Challenge

The hotel wanted guests to see every room type with prices and amenities, and to send a booking request directly from the homepage.

What I Built

  • Two-step booking request form (booking info, then personal info) on the homepage
  • Eight room and suite cards with amenities icons and prices in Naira
  • Event and conference section with pages for weddings, conferences, corporate events and parties
  • Dining, poolside and 'Why Guests Choose Us' sections
  • Rooms & Suites, Events, Airport Shuttle and Contact pages

How It Was Built

  1. Requirements and sitemap reviewed with the project team
  2. Page designs prepared and approved (Figma / PSD)
  3. Approved design built in WordPress with Elementor Pro, section by section
  4. Plugins installed and configured: WPForms (multi-step)
  5. Responsive check and fixes for desktop, tablet and mobile
  6. Speed, basic on-page SEO and cross-browser testing
  7. Demo review, client feedback round, then launch on the live domain

Tech Stack

WordPressElementor ProWPForms (multi-step)

  • Room cards with pricing
  • Event pages
