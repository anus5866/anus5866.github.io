The Balagee Foundation

A one-page style website for a family foundation supporting education, mentorship, environmental care, animal welfare and community programs.

IndustryNon-Profit / Philanthropy
LocationHoover, AL, USA
CategoryNon-Profit
My RoleWordPress Developer

The Challenge

The foundation wanted to show the real impact of its giving, with long, heartfelt testimonials from schools, police, fire departments and charities, while keeping the page easy to scan.

What I Built

  • Single-page layout with anchor navigation (About, Values, Mission, Programs, Testimonials)
  • Hero slider and founder & history section
  • Five program blocks with icons
  • Animated value progress bars
  • Testimonial carousel and contact form with a 'Donation Opportunity' subject option

How It Was Built

  1. Requirements and sitemap reviewed with the project team
  2. Page designs prepared and approved (Figma / PSD)
  3. Approved design built in WordPress with Elementor Pro, section by section
  4. Plugins installed and configured: Jeg Elementor Kit, Contact Form
  5. Responsive check and fixes for desktop, tablet and mobile
  6. Speed, basic on-page SEO and cross-browser testing
  7. Demo review, client feedback round, then launch on the live domain
