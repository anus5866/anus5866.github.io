About Me

I studied Software Engineering at Sindh Madressatul Islam University and started building websites before I graduated. My first real role was a remote internship with Flitkick Innovation in Dubai in 2023, where I built page sections in Elementor and WPBakery and published content for UAE businesses.

In November 2023 I joined The Byte Office as an Associate Web Developer and was given responsibility for goldeneagledistributors.com, a live wholesale distributor store. I kept its inventory, stock levels and pricing accurate through the ManageMore integration, and within three months I was promoted to Senior Web Developer. From then on I owned the site's uptime, security, backups, speed, SEO and the seasonal campaign updates for US holidays.

Since November 2024 I have been a Senior WordPress Developer at Brainwave Technologies / Exotech, an agency serving US clients. Here I take projects from an approved Figma, PSD or XD design to a finished, responsive WordPress site: building the pages, setting up WooCommerce, membership, LMS, booking, events and donation features, fixing plugin conflicts, and getting each site ready for launch. Most of the 200+ websites on this portfolio were delivered in this role.
